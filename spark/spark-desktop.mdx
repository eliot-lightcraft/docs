---
title: 'Syncing composite takes'
description: 'Offload recorded takes from an iPhone or iPad and review them with your team in the browser'
---

Every take recorded in [compositing mode](/spark/compositing-and-recording) is stored on the device until it's uploaded to the platform. The Spark Desktop app moves takes and their full metadata from the device to the cloud, where the whole team can review them in the browser.

<Warning>
Takes are stored on the device until uploaded. Do not delete the Spark iOS app while it holds unuploaded takes — an app version update is safe, but deleting the app may not be.
</Warning>

## Install Spark Desktop

Spark Desktop is a lightweight companion app for Mac and Windows, downloadable from the Gallery in the Spark web app.

## Offload takes

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/1221837082" width="640" height="360" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

1. Plug the iPhone or iPad into your computer over USB-C.
2. Open Spark Desktop and choose a destination folder.
3. Pull takes from the device.

Spark Desktop locates everything on the device, pulls each take with its full metadata, organizes the local folder by production, and uploads takes to the cloud. Each recording reserves a slot in the Gallery (marked with a lightning bolt while it waits); uploading fills that slot.

## Reviewing takes in the browser

In the Gallery, the whole team can review takes organized by scene and setup. Web downloads of takes are MP4 only — the full separated file set (raw camera, composite, background plate, depth map, tracking data, and metadata) lives locally with whoever ran the Spark Desktop offload.

## Sending takes to post

The take bundle can be pushed directly into a pre-configured Nuke or Blender project. See [Autoshot overview](/autoshot/autoshot-overview) for the broader post-production pipeline once footage moves beyond Spark.

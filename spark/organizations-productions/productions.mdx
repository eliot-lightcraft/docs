From your organization page, select **New Production** and enter a name. Productions act as containers for scripts, scenes, and the Gallery.

## Choosing who can open a production

Each production has an **Access** setting. You choose it when you create the production, and you can change it later: on the organization page, open the **…** menu on the production's card and click **Settings**.

- **Everyone in the organization** — Every member can open the production.
- **Only selected people** — Admins can open it, plus the members you tick.

1. **Only selected people** shows the list of members.
2. A ticked member can open this production.
3. An **Admin** has no checkbox, because admins can open every production.

Click **Save Changes** to apply the new set. You can also set this per person from the organization's **Members** list. See [changing access after you invite](/spark/organizations-productions/organizations#changing-access-after-you-invite).

## Production dashboard overview

The production dashboard displays all productions in your organization. You can switch between grid and list views and sort by name or date.

## Navigating scripts and scenes

Within a production, use the sidebar to move between:

- **Scripts** — Open the script editor to write or review screenplays.
- **Scenes** — Access the scene editor for any scene generated from script headings.

## Deleting a production

Admins can delete a production from the production settings. Deleting a production removes all associated scripts, scenes, and Gallery content. This action cannot be undone.

## Creating an organization

To create a new organization, click **New Organization** on the Home page and enter a name. You become the owner of any organization you create, with the Admin role.

## Inviting team members

Invite collaborators to your organization by email. Invited members receive a notification and can join immediately if they already have a Spark web account.

To send an invitation, click the gear icon in the top bar, open **Organization Settings**, and click **Invite** in the **Members** section. Only the organization owner can invite.

### Choosing production access

When you invite someone as a **User**, the **Production access** section of the invite window sets which productions they can open once they accept.

<img
  src="/images/spark/invite-production-access.png"
  alt="The Invite member window with the Production access section. Two productions are ticked and one is marked Everyone."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Search productions** filters the list by name.
2. **Select all** ticks every production the search is showing.
3. The count shows how many productions are ticked.
4. A production marked **Everyone** is already open to the whole organization. It has no checkbox, because every member can open it.

One choice applies to every person in the invitation who has the User role.

If everyone in the invitation is an **Admin**, the list is replaced by a note. Admins can open every production, so there is nothing to choose.

### Changing access after you invite

A sent invitation appears in the **Members** list until the person accepts.

<img
  src="/images/spark/members-pending-invitation.png"
  alt="The Members list with a pending invitation row showing a Pending tag, a production count, and an arrow button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Pending** marks an invitation that has not been accepted yet.
2. The count is the productions this person can open after they accept. It includes productions open to everyone, so it can be higher than the number you ticked.
3. The arrow opens **Production access** for that row. Clicking anywhere on the row does the same.

In **Production access**, tick or clear productions and click **Save Changes**. This works for a pending invitation and for a member who has already joined.

<img
  src="/images/spark/production-access-window.png"
  alt="The Production access window for a member, with a list of productions and a Save Changes button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The productions this person can be given. Ticked productions are the ones they can open.
2. **Save Changes** applies the new set.

## Seats and invitations

An organization has a fixed number of seats. Each member uses one seat, and so does each invitation that has not been accepted yet.

### The seat line

Open **Organization Settings** and look under **Members**. A line shows how many seats are in use, such as **1 of 3 seats used**. An invitation that has not been accepted yet holds a seat and is counted as pending: **2 of 3 seats used · 1 pending**.

When every seat is taken, the line turns red and reads **3 of 3 seats used · 2 pending — add a paid seat from Billing to invite more**. The **Invite** button is greyed out, with the tooltip **Seat capacity reached**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/seat-line-full.png"
  alt="The Members section of Organization Settings. The seat line is red and reads 3 of 3 seats used, 2 pending, add a paid seat from Billing to invite more. The Invite button is greyed out with the tooltip Seat capacity reached. Below are the owner and two pending invitations."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The seat line of a full organization.
2. **Invite** is greyed out until a seat is free.

### The invite window

The **Invite member** window shows the number of free seats under **Invite by email**. It checks each address when you leave the box.

<img
  src="/images/spark/invite-window-free-seats.png"
  alt="The Invite member window. Under Invite by email it reads 2 free seats. The email box holds abc and shows Invalid email format under it."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The free seats left in the organization.
2. The message under an address that cannot be invited.

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Invalid email format** | The text is not an email address. |
| **Already a member** | That person is in the organization. |
| **Invitation already sent** | That address has an invitation that is still pending. |
| **Invitation expired**, with **Resend it** | That address has an invitation that ran out. Click **Resend it** to send it again. |

**Add more user** is greyed out, with the tooltip **No more free seats**, when the rows in the window already use every free seat.

### Pending invitations

An invitation appears in the **Members** list with the tag **Pending** and the time left, such as **expires in 7 days**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/pending-invitation-row.png"
  alt="The Members list with one pending invitation. The seat line reads 2 of 3 seats used, 1 pending. The invitation row shows expires in 7 days, the tag Pending, an envelope icon and a red X."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The seat line counts the invitation as pending.
2. The invitation row, with the envelope icon and the **X**.

- The envelope icon (**Resend invitation**) sends it again and shows **Invitation resent to** with the address.
- The **X** asks **Cancel this invitation?** and warns **The invite link will no longer work.** Click **Cancel invitation**. The message **Invitation cancelled!** appears, and the seat is free again.

<img
  src="/images/spark/cancel-invitation-confirm.png"
  alt="A small box over the Members list that asks Cancel this invitation? The invite link will no longer work. It has the buttons Cancel and Cancel invitation."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

### Expired invitations

An invitation that has run out moves under the heading **Expired invitations · don't hold a seat**. It has the tag **Expired** and the date it ran out. It is no longer counted on the seat line, so its seat can go to someone else.

<img
  src="/images/spark/expired-invitations-group.png"
  alt="The Members list with a group named Expired invitations, don't hold a seat. One row in it has the tag Expired and reads expired Oct 7. The seat line reads 2 of 3 seats used, 1 pending, and the Invite button is enabled."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The seat line no longer counts the expired invitation.
2. The expired invitation, under its own heading.
3. **Invite** is enabled again.

To send an expired invitation again, click the resend icon on its row, or type the address in the invite window and click **Resend it**. The invitation is **Pending** again and holds a seat.

<img
  src="/images/spark/invite-window-resend-it.png"
  alt="The Invite member window. Under the email box it reads Invitation expired, with a Resend it link beside it."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The message for an address whose invitation ran out.
2. **Resend it** sends the invitation again.

If the organization has no free seat, the resend icon is greyed out, with the tooltip **No free seat — add one from Billing to resend**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/resend-greyed-no-free-seat.png"
  alt="The Members list of a full organization. The resend icon on the expired invitation is greyed out, with the tooltip No free seat, add one from Billing to resend."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The greyed resend icon and its tooltip.
2. The seat line of the full organization.

### Accepting when the organization is full

A person who accepts an invitation while the organization has no free seat is not added. They see a page that names the organization and says it **is out of seats**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/out-of-seats-page.png"
  alt="A page that reads QA seats is out of seats. Under it: The organization needs more seats before you can join. Ask its administrator to add a seat, then try again. Your invitation stays valid until 15/10/2026. Two buttons: Try again and Go to Home."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

The page says the invitation stays valid and gives its last day. After the owner frees or adds a seat, **Try again** lets the person in.

## Roles and permissions

Organizations support the following roles:

- **Admin** — Full access to organization settings, member management, and all productions.
- **User** — Can open the productions shared with the whole organization, plus any productions they are given access to. Cannot manage organization settings or members.

## Leaving an organization

You can leave an organization at any time from the organization settings. If you are the only admin, you must assign another admin before leaving.

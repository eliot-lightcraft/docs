## Creating an organization

To create a new organization, click **New Organization** on the Home page and enter a name. You become the owner of any organization you create, with the Admin role.

## Inviting team members

Invite collaborators to your organization by email. Invited members receive a notification and can join immediately if they already have a Spark web account.

To send an invitation, click the gear icon in the top bar, open **Organization Settings**, and click **Invite** in the **Members** section. Only the organization owner can invite.

### Choosing production access

When you invite someone as a **User**, the **Production access** section of the invite window sets which productions they can open once they accept.

<img
  src="/images/spark/invite-production-access.png"
  alt="The Invite member window with the Production access section. Two productions are ticked and one is marked Everyone."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Search productions** filters the list by name.
2. **Select all** ticks every production the search is showing.
3. The count shows how many productions are ticked.
4. A production marked **Everyone** is already open to the whole organization. It has no checkbox, because every member can open it.

One choice applies to every person in the invitation who has the User role.

If everyone in the invitation is an **Admin**, the list is replaced by a note. Admins can open every production, so there is nothing to choose.

### Changing access after you invite

A sent invitation appears in the **Members** list until the person accepts.

<img
  src="/images/spark/members-pending-invitation.png"
  alt="The Members list with a pending invitation row showing a Pending tag, a production count, and an arrow button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Pending** marks an invitation that has not been accepted yet.
2. The count is the productions this person can open after they accept. It includes productions open to everyone, so it can be higher than the number you ticked.
3. The arrow opens **Production access** for that row. Clicking anywhere on the row does the same.

In **Production access**, tick or clear productions and click **Save Changes**. This works for a pending invitation and for a member who has already joined.

<img
  src="/images/spark/production-access-window.png"
  alt="The Production access window for a member, with a list of productions and a Save Changes button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The productions this person can be given. Ticked productions are the ones they can open.
2. **Save Changes** applies the new set.

## Roles and permissions

Organizations support the following roles:

- **Admin** — Full access to organization settings, member management, and all productions.
- **User** — Can open the productions shared with the whole organization, plus any productions they are given access to. Cannot manage organization settings or members.

## Leaving an organization

You can leave an organization at any time from the organization settings. If you are the only admin, you must assign another admin before leaving.

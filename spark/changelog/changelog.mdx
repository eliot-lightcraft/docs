## New

<span id="r-2026-09-30-1" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Use a modelled USD set as your location

Your location no longer has to be a Gaussian splat. Upload a `.usd`, `.usda`, `.usdc` or `.usdz` set and it loads at the scale and orientation its file declares. [Adding a USD set](/spark/scene-editor/importing-locations#adding-a-usd-set)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-2" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Cut straight from your Gallery lists

The Editor's left column now shows your production's lists and a script's All Takes, so a CG Take or Take goes onto the timeline without being imported first. [Finding takes](/spark/editing/editing#finding-takes)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-3" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Choose production access when you invite people

When you invite members as Users, pick which productions they get. You can change it later from the Members list or from Production Settings. [Choosing production access](/spark/organizations-productions/organizations#choosing-production-access)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-4" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Regenerate a take's BG

Choose **Regenerate BG** from a take's card menu to render its background again from the take's camera track. [Regenerating a background](/spark/gallery/gallery#regenerating-a-background)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-5" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Download Gallery takes as one zip

Every Gallery download now arrives as a single zip with folders for 3D scene, setup, camera and take. [Downloading a zip file](/spark/gallery/gallery#downloading-a-zip-file)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-6" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Download from a shared list

Anyone with a shared list link can download one take, a selection or the whole list. [Downloading from a shared list](/spark/gallery/gallery#downloading-from-a-shared-list)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-7" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### Hide objects per setup

The eye in the Outliner hides an object in the active setup only. [Hiding an object in one setup](/spark/scene-editor/setups-and-cameras#hiding-an-object-in-one-setup)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-8" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### See keyboard shortcuts in tooltips

When a button has a shortcut, its tooltip now shows the keys. [Keyboard shortcuts](/spark/editing/editing#keyboard-shortcuts)

<span id="r-2026-09-30-9" className="scroll-mt-28" />

### See setups, cameras and takes on scene cards

Scene cards now count a scene's setups, cameras and takes. [3D scene list](/spark/scripts/script-outline-and-3d-scene-list#3d-scene-list-sidebar)

## Changed

- <span id="r-2026-09-30-10" className="scroll-mt-28" />**Shots are now cameras and CG Takes.** Anywhere Spark used to say *shot*, it now says *camera* or *CG Take*.
- <span id="r-2026-09-30-11" className="scroll-mt-28" />**Base Setup.** The base setup is labeled **Base Setup** wherever it appears next to your other setups.
- <span id="r-2026-09-30-12" className="scroll-mt-28" />**Help button.** The headset in the top bar is now a question mark. The menu behind it is the same.
- <span id="r-2026-09-30-13" className="scroll-mt-28" />**Spark iOS app.** The web app now points you to the Spark iOS app.
- <span id="r-2026-09-30-14" className="scroll-mt-28" />**Watch tutorial.** The **Watch tutorial** button is gone from an empty organization.

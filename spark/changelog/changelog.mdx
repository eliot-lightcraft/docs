<Update label="2026-09-30" tags={["Web"]}>

### Use a modelled USD set as your location

Your location no longer has to be a Gaussian splat. Upload a `.usd`, `.usda`, `.usdc` or `.usdz` set and it loads at the scale and orientation its file declares, exports with the rest of the scene, and — if it was authored with animation — gets a clip you can place on the timeline like any object.

### Cut straight from your Gallery lists

The Editor's left column now shows your production's lists and a script's All Takes instead of a separate bin, so a CG Take or Take goes onto the timeline without being imported first. Whichever list you pick is saved on the edit, so anyone who opens it lands where you left it. You can create a list, add to it, reorder it and remove from it without leaving the Editor — it is the same list the Gallery shows, changed in the same place. Rows also have a size of their own now, in the Gallery's Appearance menu: Compact trades the tags and layer chips for more of a scene on screen.

### Share lists that play and work on a phone

On a shared list, every take with a picture plays when you hover over it and opens full screen when you click it. It shows the comp if there is one, otherwise the BG, otherwise the camera plate. Takes that only have a BG are marked **BG ONLY**. Anyone with the link can download one take, a selection or the whole list, packaged the same way as a Gallery download. On a phone the list switches to compact rows, a tap opens a take full screen, and a header links back to Spark.

### Download Gallery takes as one zip

Every Gallery download, whether one take or a selection, now arrives as a single zip with folders for 3D scene, setup, camera and take. You can also download takes that only have a BG.

### Regenerate a take's BG

Choose **Regenerate BG** from a take's card menu to render its background again from the take's camera track. The BG chip shows progress while it renders, and if the BG can't be regenerated, the menu tells you why.

### Choose production access when you invite people

When you invite members as Users, you can pick which productions they'll get, and they have access as soon as they accept. Pending invitations list what they'll grant, and you can change that until they're accepted. Each member's row in organization settings now shows which productions they can open, with a searchable list for changing it. Production settings show the same thing from the production's side. Productions open to the whole organization are marked with a globe.

### See keyboard shortcuts in tooltips

When a button has a shortcut, its tooltip now shows the keys, written for your keyboard: ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ on a Mac, Ctrl, Alt and Shift elsewhere. The 3D scene and the Editor now share the same keys:

- **Shift+↑ / Shift+↓** jumps to the start or end of either timeline. Home and End no longer move the playhead.
- **Delete** is ⌫ on a Mac and Del elsewhere.
- **Undo and redo** are ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on a Mac, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z elsewhere. On a Mac, Ctrl+Z no longer undoes, and Ctrl+Y no longer redoes anywhere.
- **Esc** cancels a placement in the asset editor instead of closing the editor.

### See setups, cameras and takes on scene cards

Scene cards on the script page, home, the 3D scene drawer and the production scene picker now count a scene's setups, cameras and takes. The counts update as you add more, and hovering over a count tells you what it includes.

### Shots are now cameras and CG Takes

Anywhere Spark used to say *shot*, it now says *camera* or *CG Take*, matching the Outliner. This covers the 3D scene, the Editor, the Gallery and shared lists. The base setup is now labeled **Base Setup** wherever it appears next to your other setups.

### Hide objects per setup

The eye in the Outliner hides objects again, and a hide now belongs to the setup you're in, so an object can be hidden in one setup and visible in another. Cloning a setup copies its hides to the clone, and CG Take previews use each camera's setup hides. Locations, locators and rulers are still hidden across the whole scene.

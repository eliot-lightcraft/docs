Scene cards now count a scene's setups, cameras and takes.

## Counts on a 3D scene card

Each 3D scene card shows three counts under the scene name.

<img
  src="/images/spark/updates/2026-09-30/scene-card-counts.png"
  alt="A 3D scene card showing three counts under its name: 1 setup, 1 camera and 1 take."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Setups** in the scene, including the Base Setup.
2. **Cameras** across all setups.
3. **Takes**: one CG Take for each camera, plus any takes you have recorded.

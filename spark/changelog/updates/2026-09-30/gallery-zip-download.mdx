Every Gallery download now arrives as a single zip with folders for 3D scene, setup, camera and take.

## Downloading a zip file

To download several cards at once, click **Select Takes**, tick the cards, and click **Download**. A selection is also saved as one zip file.

The zip file is named with the date and time, then "Gallery Takes". Its folders follow the Gallery: 3D scene, then setup, then camera.

- A CG Take is one MP4 inside its camera folder.
- A take has its own folder inside the camera folder, with one file for each layer it has. The composite is named after the take. Other layers add the layer name. Tracking data, when the take has it, is saved in the same folder.
- A take with no camera has its folder at the top of the zip file.

Downloads are useful for sharing outside the Spark web app or as input for AI video tools, where a real camera move produces cleaner output than a text prompt.

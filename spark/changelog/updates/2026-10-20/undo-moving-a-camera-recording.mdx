---
title: "Moving a camera recording can be undone"
description: "Ctrl+Z puts a moved camera recording back, together with any keyframes the move deleted"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-10-20](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-20)

After you drag a camera recording to a new time on the timeline, such as one recorded with **Direct Drive**, press **Ctrl+Z** (**⌘Z** on a Mac) to put it back. If the move overlapped keyframes and you clicked **Delete & Move**, one undo puts the recording back and brings back the deleted keyframes. Before, moving a recording could not be undone.

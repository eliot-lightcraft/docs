---
title: "With no camera selected, the preview uses the active setup's camera"
description: "The camera preview no longer picks a camera from another setup"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-10-20](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-20)

When no camera is selected, the camera preview now picks one of the active setup's cameras. Before, it could pick a camera from another setup and show that camera's view over the active setup's objects. A camera you select is still shown, whichever setup it belongs to.

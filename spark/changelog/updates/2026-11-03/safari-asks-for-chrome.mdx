---
title: "Spark asks Safari users on a Mac to switch to Chrome"
description: "After sign-in, Safari on a Mac shows a window that points to Google Chrome"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

When you sign in with Safari on a Mac, a window titled **Improve your experience** now opens. It reads **You are using Safari - it might cause the problem of crashing.** and **Please use Google Chrome to have a better experience.**, and it has a **Download Chrome** button. The window cannot be closed, so open Spark in Chrome to go on.

The window does not show on the sign-in page, in Chrome or another Chromium browser, or on an iPhone or iPad.

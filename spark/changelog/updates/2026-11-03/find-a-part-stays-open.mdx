---
title: "Find a part stays open after a Shift-click"
description: "Shift-click several results in Find a part without opening the list again"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

**Find a part** on an object's **Parts** row now stays open when you **Shift**-click a result, so you can add several parts in a row. **Shift**-click a selected part again to take it out of the selection. A click without **Shift** selects only that part and closes the list. With the cursor in the search box, **Esc** closes the list and keeps the parts you selected. Before, the list closed after every **Shift**-click.

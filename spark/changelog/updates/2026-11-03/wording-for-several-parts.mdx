---
title: "Right wording when several parts are selected"
description: "The help text says rotate and scale work on several parts, and a count of one reads 1 part"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

With two or more parts selected, the help text in the properties panel now reads **Shift-click a part to add or remove it. Drag the gizmo to move, rotate or scale them together.** Before, it said the gizmo could only move them, although **Rotate** and **Scale** worked too.

The message after a drag now uses the right word for one part, such as **1 part moved**, **1 part rotated**, or **1 part scaled**. Before, it read **1 parts moved**. This happens when only one of the selected parts can move, for example when the other one follows a skeleton.

---
title: "Send a new confirmation link when you sign up"
description: "The Check your email screen after sign-up can send a new link, and signing in before you confirm says what to do"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

After you create an account with your email, the **Check your email** screen reads **We sent a confirmation link to** your address, followed by **Open it on any device to finish creating your account.** It now has a **Send a new link** button.

- The button counts down for a minute after each email, such as **Send a new link (42s)**. When it reaches zero, you can press it.
- After it sends, it reads **Sent. Use the link in the newest email.** Only the newest email's link works.

If you try to sign in before you confirm your address, Spark reads **Confirm your email first. We sent you a link when you signed up.**

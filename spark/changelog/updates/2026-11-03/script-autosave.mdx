Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

The script editor now saves your edits about 1.5 seconds after you stop typing. While you keep typing, it saves at least every 5 seconds. Before, it saved only 10 seconds after you stopped, and showed no save status.

## The save status

The status sits beside **Versioning** in the right column, in Edit.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Saving…**, with **Save now** | An edit is being saved. Click **Save now** to save at once. |
| **Saved** | Every edit is saved. When the saved text differs from the published script, **Publish changes** shows beside it. |
| **Couldn't save — retrying**, with **Retry now** | A save failed, and Spark tries again. |
| **Saving stopped**, with **Reload** | The editor cannot save your latest edits. Copy anything you need before you reload, because it has not been saved. |

Saving does not publish. The script everyone reads, and the 3D scenes, lists, and downloads that use it, change only when you publish.

## Version cards

Your saves share one card in **Versioning** until you pause for 5 minutes. The card shows how many saves it holds, such as **By Maya Chen · 8 saves**. After the pause, your next save starts a new card. A long run of saves with no pause also starts a new card after 30 minutes.

## Two tabs

When the same script is open in Edit in two browser tabs, typing in one shows in the other. After someone publishes in one tab, the other tab stops offering **Publish changes** for the same edits.

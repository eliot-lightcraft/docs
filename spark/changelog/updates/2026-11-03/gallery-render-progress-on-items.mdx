---
title: "Render progress shows on each Gallery item"
description: "An item whose preview is rendering shows its own progress"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

While a preview renders, its item in the Gallery now shows how far it has got: **Queued**, **Preparing…**, **Rendering** with a percentage such as **Rendering 42%**, and then **Saving…**. A background that renders shows its layer name, such as **Rendering BG 42%**.

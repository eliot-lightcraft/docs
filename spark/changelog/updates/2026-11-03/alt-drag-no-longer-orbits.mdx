---
title: "Alt-drag no longer orbits the 3D view"
description: "Orbit an object in the 3D scene by holding F after you frame it"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

In the 3D scene, **Alt**+drag no longer orbits the view. To orbit, select an object, press **F** to frame it, and keep **F** held while you drag with the left mouse button. See [press F to jump to the selection](/spark/changelog/updates/2026-11-03/jump-to-the-selection).

Holding **F** and dragging with a camera selected no longer orbits that camera. Before, it orbited the selected camera itself.

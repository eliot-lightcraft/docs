---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

The gear (**Settings**) on the 3D canvas toolbar now opens a menu with two entries. **Camera & controls** opens the move, look, boost, and transform size settings that the gear opened before. **Rendering…** opens the Rendering panel. See [render quality settings for splats](/spark/changelog/updates/2026-11-03/render-quality-settings).

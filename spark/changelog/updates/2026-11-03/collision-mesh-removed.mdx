---
title: "Locations no longer take a collision mesh"
description: "The collision mesh option is gone from location uploads, the Outliner, and the properties panel"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

A location no longer takes a collision mesh. The **Collision Mesh (Optional)** section is gone from the location upload window, the Outliner no longer lists a **Collider** under a location, and a location's panel is always **Location Properties**. A collision mesh only drew a wireframe in the 3D canvas and had no other effect.

---
title: "Replace a clip on the timeline"
description: "Swap a clip for other material in the same place, by dragging onto it or with the Replace key"
---

Feature · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

In the Editor you can now replace a clip on the timeline with another take, CG Take, or audio file. The new clip starts where the old one did, and nothing else on the timeline moves.

## Dragging onto a clip

1. Drag a take from the left column over a clip on the timeline.
2. The clip's place is outlined, with an amber **Replace** tag. The part of it that the new material will fill is tinted.
3. Let go.

The new clip takes the old clip's place. The clips before and after it stay where they are.

{/* PICTURE TO TAKE (p1-editor-replace-drag): The Editor timeline of Scene 12 rough cut while a take is dragged over a longer clip, showing the amber Replace tag and the tinted part */}

1. The amber **Replace** tag on the clip being replaced.
2. The tinted part is how much of the place the new material fills.

## Shorter or longer material

- If the new material is shorter than the old clip, it ends early and leaves a gap. A message gives the length of the gap, such as **Shorter than the clip it replaced, leaving a gap of 6.71s.**
- If it is longer, it is cut to the old clip's length.

If you marked a stretch of the take, only the marked stretch is used. See [mark in and out on a take in the Editor](/spark/changelog/updates/2026-10-12/editor-mark-in-and-out).

A picture clip replaces a picture clip, and a sound clip replaces a sound clip.

## Replacing at the playhead

1. Move the timeline playhead onto the clip you want to replace, on the target lane.
2. Load the new material in the **Clip** pane.
3. Press **R**, or click **Replace** beside **Insert** and **Overwrite**. Its tooltip reads **Replace the clip at the playhead on the target lane**.

If no clip is under the playhead on the target lane, Spark shows **No clip under the playhead on the target lane. Park the playhead on the clip to replace.** and changes nothing.

One **Ctrl+Z** puts the old clip back.

For the target lane, see [where the clip goes](/spark/changelog/updates/2026-10-12/editor-insert-and-overwrite#where-the-clip-goes).

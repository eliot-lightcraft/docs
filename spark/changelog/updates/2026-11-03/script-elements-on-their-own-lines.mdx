---
title: "Script elements go in as their own paragraph"
description: "Dialogue, Parenthetical, and Transition added with Add elements are no longer read as action"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

An element you add with **Add elements**, such as **Dialogue**, **Parenthetical**, or **Transition**, now goes in with one blank line before and after it. With the cursor in the middle of a paragraph, the element goes in after that paragraph. Before, the element could land straight under a paragraph and was read as action.

---
title: "The Scene navigation drawer opens above the camera preview"
description: "A large camera preview no longer covers the Scene navigation drawer"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-11-03](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-11-03)

In a 3D scene, the **Scene navigation** drawer now opens on top of the camera preview, and you can click every scene card in it. Before, a large camera preview covered part of the open drawer and took the clicks there.

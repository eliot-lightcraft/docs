Feature · Web · Released [2026-10-05](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-05)

The help menu in the top bar has a new item, **What's new**.

Click the **?** icon in the top bar (tooltip **Help & support**). The menu now has four items:

1. **Report an issue**
2. **Join our Discord**
3. **Get Lightcraft Spark for iPhone**
4. **What's new**

**What's new** opens the [releases](/spark/changelog/changelog) page in a new browser tab. Your Spark tab stays where it was.

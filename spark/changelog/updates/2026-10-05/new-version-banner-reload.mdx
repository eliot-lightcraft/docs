---
title: "Reload on the new version banner keeps your place"
description: "The Reload button on the new version banner no longer brings the banner back"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-10-05](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-05)

When a new version of Spark is out, a banner at the top of the page reads **A new version is available**, with a **Reload** button. **Reload** now loads the same page you were on, and the banner no longer comes back after the reload.

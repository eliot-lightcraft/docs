---
title: "The Editor shows a take's new files without a reload"
description: "A take's new background or upload now reaches an open Editor"
---

Fix · Web · Released [2026-10-12](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-12)

When a take gets a new background or a new upload while the Editor is open, the Editor now uses the new files without a reload. Before, the Editor kept the take's old files until you reloaded the page.

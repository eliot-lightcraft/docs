title: "Raw depth is a download chip"
description: "A take whose depth was saved in the raw format now has a Depth (raw) chip that downloads the file"
---

Change · Web · Released [2026-10-12](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-12)

A take whose depth was saved in the phone's raw format now has a **Depth (raw)** chip on its card, beside **Tracking**. Click it to download the file. Its tooltip ends with **16-bit depth, lossless**. Before, such a take showed a dashed **Depth** chip, as if the depth were missing.

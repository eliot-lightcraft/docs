Change · Web · Released [2026-10-12](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-12)

The wide logo now reads **LIGHTCRAFT** above a smaller **SPARK**, beside the same bolt. It shows in the top bar, on the sign-in page, in the header of a shared list, and in Spark's emails for confirming your address, invitations, and password resets. The logo in the top bar is a little larger.

The square logo and the browser tab icon are not changed.

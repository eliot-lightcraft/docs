Change · Web · Released [2026-10-12](/spark/changelog/changelog#2026-10-12)

**Regenerate BG** in a take card's **…** menu now renders a background only for a take that has none. Once a take has a background, the menu item is greyed out, and its tooltip reads **This take already has a background**.

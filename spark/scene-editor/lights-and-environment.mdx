---
title: 'Scene lighting'
description: 'Every 3D scene uses the same fixed studio lighting.'
---

The light comes from one baked HDRI, Lightcraft's **Spark Studio** environment. You can't pick another environment, so a scene looks the same in the Spark web app and the Spark iOS app.

## The fixed studio lighting

- The Outliner has no **Lights** row. You can't add a directional, spot or point light.
- You can't change the lighting's brightness.
- The lighting falls on USD objects only. A Gaussian splat keeps the baked lighting from its scan or render.

Lightcraft is working on adjustable lighting.

<Tip>
  To change the look, bake it into the asset. Light the splat before you scan or render it, or give a USD object its own material.
</Tip>
